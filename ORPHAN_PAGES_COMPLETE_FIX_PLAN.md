**Sitemap vs Internal Links:**

- **Sitemap = discovery:** Tells Google URLs exist, but does NOT pass PageRank, does NOT help user navigation
- **Internal links = authority + crawl efficiency:** Passes PageRank, helps Googlebot find pages via crawling, helps users navigate, builds topical authority

**You need BOTH.** Putting page in sitemap does NOT replace internal links. That's why 1,170 pages are in sitemap (referenced) but Ahrefs shows 0 inlinks — they are discovered via sitemap but not via internal links.
